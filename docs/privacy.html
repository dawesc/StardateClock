Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-31

Overview

Stardate Clock is a watchOS utility that calculates and displays a fictional stardate.

Data Collection

Stardate Clock does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not track users across apps or websites.

On-device Data

The app stores your selected stardate era locally on your device using Apple-provided app storage so the complication can read the same setting.

Third Parties

The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party tracking SDKs.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@thedawes.me
